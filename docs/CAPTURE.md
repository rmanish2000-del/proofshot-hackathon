# Seller capture guide (SPEC-composite-v2 §9)

The composite v2 product shot uses only your own photo's pixels. It can even
out light and colour a little (within recorded bounds), but it cannot fix a
photo that is cut off, burnt out or in hard sun. When a photo is not good
enough, the job is REVIEW with one of the reasons below and no image. Retake
the photo following the step the reason links to.

## 1. Background

Hang the garment on a hanger against a plain light wall, or lay it flat on a
plain sheet. A busy background can break the garment mask into pieces.

Reason that links here: "mask is fragmented".

## 2. Light

Shoot in open shade or by a window, never in direct sun. No shadow bands across
the garment. Do not let white parts burn out to pure white, or dark parts sink
to pure black.

Reasons that link here: "harsh shadow across the garment; retake in shade",
"over- or under-exposed photo", "correction too large".

## 3. Smooth

Flatten wrinkles (smooth by hand or steam). Button it up and straighten the
sleeves.

## 4. Framing

Fill most of the frame and keep the whole garment inside the photo. Camera at
chest height, straight on.

Reasons that link here: "garment fills too little of the photo; retake
closer", "garment is cut off at the edge of the photo", "photo resolution too
low".

## 5. Front and back

Take a front photo and a back photo, with no people, faces or hands in the
frame.
